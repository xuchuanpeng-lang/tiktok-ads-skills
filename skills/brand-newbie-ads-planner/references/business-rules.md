# Business rules and applicability

Brand Newbie Ads Planner helps registered new TikTok advertisers set up their first Brand Ads campaign according to Best Practice. It supports Traffic, Reach and Video Views, from objectives, budgets, targeting, assets and schedules through confirmation, creation and configuration verification.

## Rule types

- Newcomer identification and conversation: use account-history evidence, recommend Traffic first, and introduce Reach / Video Views by exposure/viewing intent after an explicit no-URL statement.
- Objective Best Practice: map MCP registered country to one of five regions, then select the budget by Objective. See planning.md for quantities and duration. Amounts are USD references; verify currency, platform minimums and user limits.
- Q4 Traffic incentive: a business program with dates and eligibility conditions. See onboarding.md. Explaining the program does not confirm account eligibility, available slots or payout.
- Country mapping: use advertiser_country and Region (5-group) in advertiser-country-region.csv. Keep blank regions unknown; do not add inferred regions or unverified aliases.
- MCP connection: verify tools, authorization and account access before account operations. If unavailable, follow mcp-connection.md to guide configuration and authorization in the user's agent, then validate through read-only requests.
- Platform creation requirements: follow current TT4B schemas, permissions and applicable official documentation. Best Practice and incentive conditions are not universal creation requirements.

## Verification

Verify current platform facts through available tools or official documentation. Establish incentive qualification from valid program terms and actual account data. Do not extend the 2026 program into a future quarter automatically.
