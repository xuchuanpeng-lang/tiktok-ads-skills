# Newcomer identification and first-campaign guidance

## Evidence for newcomer status

Classify at advertising-account level only when either condition is established:

1. Registered, no first spend: no Campaign creation history of any type, and lifetime actual cost = 0.
2. Recently first spent in Q4: the true first-spend date falls within October 1–December 31, 2026, and no Campaign was running during the seven-day window preceding verification.

Record evidence, coverage and timezone. Cover all lifecycle states and pagination; a currently visible or Brand-only Campaign list is insufficient. Zero spend over seven days does not prove no running Campaign. Creation date or the earliest row in a short report is not the true first-spend date. Current status cannot establish historical activity. If tools cannot establish these facts, keep classification unknown and continue independent planning rather than repeatedly requesting unavailable evidence.

This classification does not establish incentive eligibility. Account registration is outside scope.

## Conversation rules

- No explicit Objective: first recommend Traffic, briefly explain the applicable incentive and ask for the promotion URL. Do not present a three-Objective menu in the opening question.
- URL already supplied: reuse it, while checking suitability and accessibility.
- No answer to the URL question is not an explicit no-URL statement.
- Explicitly no URL: explain that Reach helps more distinct people see a brand/product/activity, while Video Views helps people watch a demonstration or story. Recommend from the stated exposure/viewing goal; ask only if unclear.
- Honor a supported explicit request, including Reach, Video Views or Traffic LPV; do not force Traffic Clicks.
- Do not apply the Traffic incentive to Reach or Video Views or promise sales.

Opening example when no URL is known:

> I suggest starting with Traffic to bring visits to your website or product page. Under the 2026 Q4 program rules, qualifying Web Traffic spend can earn 50% in ad credit, capped at USD 100 per advertising account, with no sign-up required. Creative and active-delivery conditions apply, and eligibility and available slots still need verification. Which page would you like to promote? Please send the URL.

Explicit no-URL example:

> You can still start without a promotion URL. Reach helps more people see your brand or activity. Video Views helps people watch a product demonstration or brand story. Would you prefer broader exposure or more viewing of your content?

Use these only when the relevant questions remain unanswered.

## 2026 Q4 Traffic incentive rules

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Audience | Global; quarterly new and reactivated advertisers, subject to program eligibility |
| Enrollment | No sign-up required |
| Slots | 2,000, first come first served; this package does not establish current availability |
| Objective | Web Traffic only |
| Bidding | Applicable Maximum Delivery automatic bidding during ramp-up |
| Creatives | At least 6, including 2 images; formats and placements must support them |
| Activity | At least 14 actual active delivery days within 30 days; scheduling 14 days does not establish completion |
| Qualifying spend window | November 5–December 31, 2026 |
| Reward | Ad credit equal to 50% of qualifying spend, capped at USD 100 per Advertiser ID |
| Payout | Qualifying ad credit assigned to the TTAM advertising account before January 10, 2027 |

Program rules do not establish an account's current eligibility, available slots or actual payout. Explain conditions; only verified data can establish completion or receipt. A conditional estimate may use `min(qualifying USD spend × 50%, USD 100)`. Do not calculate an actual reward without verifying qualifying spend and any currency conversion. Deposits, configured budgets and total account spend are not automatically qualifying spend. Ad credit is not cash or an instant discount.

Verify the program's day-boundary timezone, treatment of active days across windows and full eligibility terms. Near the deadline, check remaining countable days, current terms and timezone; a schedule alone cannot guarantee qualification. Do not invent a consecutive-14-day condition. After expiry, describe historical rules or report historical results only; do not promote an expired incentive as currently available. Confirm scope for subsequent-quarter requests.
