# Example requests

## General entry

> Help me prepare my Brand Ads Black Friday advertising. Review my account and recommend a plan before changing anything.

## Historical plans

> Review all running campaigns promoting [URL / brand / activity] in [countries]. Tell me which can continue, propose supported budget increases, and suggest how many creatives to add. Do not change anything yet.

> Show the exact Campaign and Ad Group IDs, current budgets and proposed budgets for this batch.

> Explain the BAU calculation, Benchmark matching row, covered creative phases and recommended lower bound.

## New plans


> Prepare a new Traffic campaign for [URL] in [countries], following applicable Best Practice. Use my account history where relevant and show the plan first.

> Prepare a new Reach campaign for [brand/activity] in [countries]. Recommend the configuration and available assets before creating it.

> Prepare a Video Views campaign using [assets]. Recommend 6-second or 15-second Focused View and explain the choice.



## Registered new advertiser

> I have a registered TikTok ad account and want to start advertising. Recommend a starting plan and explain the Traffic incentive. Do not create ads yet.

## Multiple directions and reporting

> Help with Reach first, then Traffic. Treat [amount/currency] as the shared activity budget including existing plans, and complete one direction at a time.

> Give me a brief report for [dates] on [Objective and scope]. Keep 6-second and 15-second Focused View results separate and explain any missing data.

Bracketed information is optional context, not an onboarding form that must be filled in full. Reuse known values and ask only consequential missing questions. An explicit new request does not require completing historical optimization first.
