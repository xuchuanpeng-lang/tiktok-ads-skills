# Account entry and task routing

## Scope and access

Use current TT4B tools; read `tools.md`. Check connection, account access, and promoted scope at entry. Do not query every creative, Pixel, or creation field before choosing the task. Missing connection: explain the host's supported TT4B connection/authorization flow and continue with known information; do not pretend account analysis happened.

`advertiser_id` identifies an advertising account, not an internal MMM/CRM customer or agency record. A future MMM-keyed source needs an explicit mapping. Business Center ownership does not establish account-management responsibilities.

For a vague request, read seven complete available days in a stated timezone. Include Reach, Traffic and Video Views activity through supported reads/reports. Do not present shares over a partial dataset as full-account shares.

| Overview field | Handling |
|---|---|
| Objective and destination | Preserve actual configuration; Reach/Traffic/Video Views are this skill’s supported Brand Objectives |
| Actual spend | Same period/currency; disclose missing segments |
| Campaign count | Distinct stable Campaign IDs, not Ad Groups or report rows |
| Spend share | Objective spend / covered account spend; no zero denominator or unexplained mixed currency |
| Promoted object | Traffic/Web URL; Reach/Video Views brand/activity/assets |
| Countries | Actual target countries, not advertiser registration country |

Suggest direction from related object/country history, recent plans and spend. Largest spend is evidence, not automatic intent. Confirm direction and promoted scope together **before deep AOS**. Reuse already supplied values. Changed scope means filter against that scope, not finish diagnosing a discarded one. Many objects can be grouped for display; grouping is not a candidate cutoff.

Example: “Over the last seven complete reporting days, Traffic accounted for 60% of spend across 8 campaigns, promoting these pages in the UK and Germany. For BFCM, should we start with Traffic and continue these URLs and countries? Tell me only what you would like to change.” Use actual account values only.

Explicit new creation skips mandatory historical adjustment. Explicit review-only ends with findings. “First identify plans, then adjust” is a combined task with a separately confirmed write. Multiple Objectives form a sequential queue, carrying known fields and committed shared budget forward.

## Registered new advertisers

Classify at advertising-account level only when reliable evidence establishes either:

1. Registered, no first spend: **no Campaign creation history of any kind AND lifetime cost = 0**.
2. Recently first spent: **true first-spend date is in 2026 Q4 (October 1–December 31) AND no running Campaign during the previous seven-day window**.

An empty current undeleted list cannot prove “never created.” Cover all accessible Campaign creation history, including deleted history where available; Brand-only report coverage cannot prove lifetime account history. Recent zero spend does not prove “no active campaigns” or “never spent.” Creation date or first date seen in a short report is not first-spend date. Current statuses alone may not establish a historical window. Missing evidence keeps classification unknown; still prepare supported work. Registration/account opening itself is outside this skill.

### Progressive conversation

1. First recommend **Traffic only**, briefly introduce the incentive, and ask for the promotion URL. Do not offer Reach/Video Views in the first question. Already supplied URL: reuse it.
2. An unanswered URL question is not “no URL.” Only an explicit no-URL statement, including one given earlier, opens alternatives.
3. Explain outcomes: **Reach** helps more distinct people see the brand/product/activity; **Video Views** helps get the selected Focused View watching outcome for a demo, usage explanation or story. Ask which matters only if unclear; otherwise recommend from the stated need. Neither guarantees purchases or inherits the Traffic incentive.
4. Prepare that Objective using registered-country Best Practice. Missing BAU does not imply a zero budget/zero creative target. Respect an explicit supported Objective request instead of forcing a switch to Traffic.

First reply example: “I suggest starting with Traffic to bring visits to your website or product page. The 2026 Q4 Traffic incentive requires no sign-up; qualifying spend can earn 50% in ad credit, capped at $100 per advertising account. Which page would you like to promote? Please send the URL.” Add a brief conditions/source note; do not advertise only the reward.

No-URL reply: “You can still advertise without a URL. Reach helps more people see your brand or activity. Video Views is better suited to getting people to watch a product demo or story. Would you prefer broader exposure, or more viewing of your content?”

### 2026 Q4 Traffic Incentive Plan — business-provided rules

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Region/audience | Global; quarterly new and reactivated advertisers subject to program eligibility |
| Enrollment | **No sign-up required**, confirmed by the business owner |
| Slots | 2,000, first come first served; current availability is not established by the skill |
| Objective | Web Traffic only |
| Bidding | Applicable Maximum Delivery automatic bidding in ramp-up |
| Creatives | At least 6 including 2 images; formats/placements must support them |
| Activity | At least 14 active delivery days within 30 days; schedule alone is not proof |
| Qualifying spending | November 5–December 31, 2026; different from BFCM |
| Credit | 50% of qualifying spend, cap USD 100 per Advertiser ID; ad credit, not cash or instant discount |
| Payout | Qualifying credit assigned to the TTAM ad account **before January 10, 2027** |

The skill's new-advertiser label does not verify program eligibility, slots or actual credit. No sign-up does not mean verified entitlement. Late BFCM adoption may leave fewer than 14 days: explain the separate program window. Do not extend dates, add budget or upload assets to pursue rewards without a concrete confirmed plan. Missing an incentive condition does not make a supported campaign uncreatable.
